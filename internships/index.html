Quant Internships

This page is for students of the Quantitative Finance program at Stony Brook University. It lists firms that posted quantitative internship roles for Summer 2027 (quant research, trading and technology) at hedge funds, proprietary trading firms, market makers, banks and asset managers. Only PhD and master’s level roles in the United States are included.

The third column names any Summer 2027 role still open, linking straight to the posting. Where it reads Closed, the round has finished, and the firm name links to its careers page, where the next cycle will appear. An updated list for Summer 2028 is expected in August or September 2027.

If you know someone at a firm on this list, apply through them. A referral carries your application past the initial screen, which is where most applications stop.

Where a firm lists several roles, how many to apply for depends on the firm. At a fund, one application to the role that fits you best is usually enough: recruiting is centralised and the team decides where to route you. Banks are the opposite — they often state outright that you may submit two or three applications, and their programs are separate enough that the applications do not compete with one another.

The list was compiled by Matvei Lukianov from public job postings. Write to matvei.lukianov@stonybrook.edu if you know of an internship that is missing here, if you spot an error, if a role listed as open has in fact closed, or if you would like advice on applying.

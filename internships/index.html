Quant Internships

This page is for students of the Quantitative Finance program at Stony Brook University. It lists firms that recruited quantitative finance interns for Summer 2027 — quant research, trading, and technology roles at hedge funds, proprietary trading firms, market makers, banks and asset managers.

The third column names any Summer 2027 role still open, linking straight to the posting. Where it reads Closed the round has finished, and the firm name links to its careers page, where the next cycle will appear. An updated list for Summer 2028 is expected in August or September 2027.

The list was compiled by Matvei Lukianov from his own application cycle. If you know of an internship that is missing here, or you would like advice on applying, write to matvei.lukianov@stonybrook.edu.
